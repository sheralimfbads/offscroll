How are selections made?

Hacker News and Wikipedia use source-specific attention signals and may show related coverage. YouTube has its own independent selection, with at most one video per channel. Attention and shared coverage are not proof of a claim.

When should I come back?

The intended rhythm is Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Each edition considers the last seven days, so some selections may repeat. The date above always identifies the prepared edition; updates depend on successful collection and publication.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open this site in your mobile browser and choose “Add to Home Screen” from the browser menu or share sheet. It opens like a small reading app. An internet connection is required for new editions and original sources.

Privacy & your saved selections

No accounts, analytics, or visitor AI requests. Save, read and appearance choices stay in this browser and are never sent to us. Saved/read choices expire after 30 days; only selections still in this edition are shown. Hosting providers have their own request logs.

Loading a YouTube player connects to Google's privacy-enhanced player and shares your IP address and site origin. YouTube may store data and show ads; its own privacy policy applies. Offscroll does not copy titles, thumbnails or videos. Direct links take you to the original sites.
